Report a Problem

Been cheated, seen a fake listing or a suspicious offer? Tell us here and the AgroLink team will look into it.

Stay safe when trading

Always call the farmer or buyer before paying. Meet in a busy market place, check the produce yourself, and avoid sending money to anyone you have not spoken to.

What happens to your report

The AgroLink team reads every report in the admin inbox. Listings found to be fake or dishonest are removed from the marketplace.

Urgent cases

If you have lost money or feel unsafe, also report the matter to the Nigeria Police in your local government area.
